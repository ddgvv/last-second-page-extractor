Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Client-Side Processing

At EasyTool.in, we prioritize your document security. Most of our PDF tools (Merge, Split, Unlock) perform processing locally in your browser. This means your files are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring 100% privacy for your sensitive documents.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require registration or personal information to use our tools. However, like most websites, we collect non-identifiable data such as browser type, IP address, and pages visited to improve our service.

We use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google uses cookies (like the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
